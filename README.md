# My Favorite Series 
## Game of Thrones 

 is an HBO fantasy drama (2011-2019) based on George R.R. Martin's novel series *A Song of Ice and Fire*. Set in the continents of Westeros and Essos, it follows noble families such as the Starks, Lannisters, and Targaryens as they scheme, fight, and ally in a brutal struggle for the Iron Throne and rule over the Seven Kingdoms.

While these houses fight over power, an ancient threat gathers in the far North: the White Walkers and their army of the dead. The show is known for its huge cast, political intrigue, shocking deaths, epic battles, and morally gray characters, with dragons and magic woven into a world that otherwise feels grounded and realistic.
